# What the first five discovery screens taught us

Interim scientific review, 1 October 2026. The campaign is still active. These
are results of controlled models, not observations or five discoveries. This
note accompanies the [portable checkpoint](../../REPORT.md); the new bar-growth
ensemble and cuspy feedback extension have no outcome in this release.

## Hidden populations: remove the confounder analytically

The first finite orbit search preserved density and even moments but left
small local-streaming differences. Its largest apparent effects also needed
new sampling and phase checks. A better construction cancels streaming
analytically at every position, while an exact rational positivity argument
keeps both distributions above half the reference DF. This replaces a
finite-bin constraint with a model identity.

The fixed p = 8 pair then gives a resolved imposed-bar response difference at
the reference frequency and passes the selected phase and timestep screens.
Its transfer ratio is about 1.45 as a ratio of point estimates. The initial
factor-of-two ambition has not been reached; collective stability, a softened
live equilibrium and live bar evolution remain separate questions. Higher odd
velocity moments provide an intuitive view of the hidden population structure,
but are not by themselves a torque predictor. See
[the exact-twin readback](PUBLIC_TWINS_EXACT_SUMMARY.md).

The p = 4 phase check fails; the p = 6 supplementary sampling margin is smaller
than its largest measured phase shift. The second frequency has no phase
refinement. Those qualifications prevent the strongest reference result from
being read as a guarantee for every population or a new forcing history.

## Heating: a good global ratio can hide a bad local cost

A high-frequency gas pulse preferentially heats a stationary central halo
tracer in a cored model. Direct evolution and numerical refinements support
that restricted example. But a whole-star normalization gives a cost of 0.00338,
whereas the innermost fixed guiding cohort gives 0.08996. Distant stars dilute
the average. The earlier frequency-5 waveform gives a much larger inner cost,
so the frequency dependence remains useful; the global ratio is not a
co-spatial observational bound.

The model's central stellar hole and maximum circular frequency matter. An
independent test with a cuspy halo and centrally populated stars is now in
progress. The cored frequency gap cannot be assumed to survive that change;
no cuspy outcome is available here. See
[the heating summary](FEEDBACK_PUBLIC_SUMMARY.md).

## Echoes: demonstrate memory, then ask whether gravity can read it

Independent calculations and erased-memory controls support a radial halo
echo. Its gravitational readout is only about 22 parts per million of the
background force at the stronger frozen amplitude. An illustrative isolated
stellar response is too small to support visible structure. A potential
variation can also be spatially constant inside an outer spherical shell,
which makes force a necessary observable. A separate spatial two-frequency
prediction is even smaller and lacks finite-amplitude trajectory confirmation.

Connecting this phase-space memory to visible galactic structure would require
a separately tested collective amplifier. See
[the echo summary](PUBLIC_ECHO_SUMMARY.md).

## Collisions: match the operator before claiming a discriminator

Matching scalar viscosity left the conditional drift and diffusion tensor
different. A cleaner elastic-law pair matches both first and second
conditional velocity coefficients while retaining a different higher moment.
Nevertheless, its moving-wave contrast does not retain a qualified sign under
the selected timestep and cell refinements. That is a legitimate stop decision.
It is not evidence that all collision laws are equivalent or a result for a
responsive halo.

## Modified inertia: circular calibration leaves other motions undetermined

Published harmonic equations pass their known limits and residual checks.
Different recorded frequency filters with the same circular normalization
give substantially different secondary frequencies. The sharp-spectrum
degeneracy, general admissibility and causal evolution remain unresolved;
no observed motions select among these filters. This is a useful falsification
screen, not a replacement theory of gravity. The two terminal branches are
summarized [together](PUBLIC_TERMINAL_BRANCHES.md).

## Numerical lessons shared by the screens

Independent whole randomized libraries are different from particles in an
adaptive quasi-random sample. Phase quadrature, timestep convergence and
sampling precision need separate records. Refinement should be assessed
against the small contrast being interpreted, rather than a much larger
initial action or energy scale.

A correct force implementation can still have an unphysical source. Positive
gas density and finite flow energy are distinct constraints. A
finite-time weak forecast can differ from a direct response because of finite
amplitude, quadrature or a population integration-by-parts identity that does
not hold family by family. These are distinct explanations, tested separately.

Public replay of scalar contractions and exact algebra makes the evidence
inspectable. It does not repeat the trajectories, establish confidence
coverage or validate the models against galaxies. The useful next computation
should remove a consequential physical or numerical ambiguity.
