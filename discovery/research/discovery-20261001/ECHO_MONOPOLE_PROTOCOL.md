# Frozen spherical halo-memory pilot

Defined 1 October 2026 before the first monopole response run. This is a cheaper separate screen after the spatial-quadrupole result remained unresolved. It is a selected-halo radial-memory and gravitational-readout experiment, not disk validation or a new basic echo mechanism.

## Physical model and hypothesis

The potential remains the spherical isochrone with G=M=1, b=0.5. The initial DF is the isotropic reference halo DF multiplied by a smooth energy taper: zero for binding energy E≤0.02, one for E≥0.04, with a C-infinity transition. This defines an equilibrium inner-halo cohort before forcing. Its absolute reference-halo mass fraction is measured; it is never normalized to the full halo. The unselected halo response remains untested.

Both pulses are positive Plummer mass impulses with integrated potential

```
psi_i(r) = -a_i / sqrt(r^2+s_i^2),
Delta v = -a_i x / (r^2+s_i^2)^(3/2).
```

Use s_A=0.5, s_B=1.5, pulse times 0 and tau. Freeze tau=16 and24 and equal amplitudes a=0.001,0.002,0.004. These impulses preserve the full L vector. They contain multiple radial harmonics, verified before interpreting responses. Their physical realization is a short-lived positive central mass contribution; finite-duration and self-gravity checks would be later gates.

Spherical forcing and spherical readout allow inclination and orbital-plane orientation to be integrated exactly. The surviving radial frequency is Omega_r(E)=(2E)^(3/2); its broad distribution is retained. Angular reduction is an exact consequence of symmetry, not a claim that generic quadrupoles have one dispersive frequency.

## Predeclared readout and controls

Measure the signed four-case mixed Newtonian potential `Phi_AB-Phi_A-Phi_B+Phi_0` averaged over lognormal annuli at R=0.5,1,2 with log-width0.1. Keep the primary R=1 potential in absolute reference-halo units. An independent observable is the smooth enclosed mass across the same annular weight. Both can drive radial stellar motions/rings in principle; no live stellar response is inferred.

The harmonic refocusing channels include n_A=1,n_B=2 at2tau and n_A=2,n_B=3 at3tau. Generic mass pulses also contain other harmonics, so an isolated bump at either time is insufficient. Freeze even/odd Gaussian time windows of width tau/4 around2tau and3tau. Require pulse-product scaling, separation dependence and memory erasure in addition to numerical convergence. The first-pulse coarse response must actually fade before claiming an apparently relaxed halo.

Forward trajectories use exact background action/angle propagation. The independent Eulerian calculation integrates the initial DF evaluated on backward histories with inverse kicks. For the memory-erased control, first project the post-A DF onto its radial-angle average at fixed new Jr,L. This preserves the action marginal and full L orientation. No simple permutation of a deterministic angle set substitutes for this projection.

## Support and numerical contract

Use the analytic bound `|Delta E| ≤ sqrt(2)|Delta v|max + |Delta v|max^2/2`, with `|Delta v|max=2a/(3 sqrt(3)s^2)`, to prove the selected cohort remains bound for the largest pulses. Record the minimum possible binding energy after both impulses. The inverse-kick calculation must audit invalid actions, support crossings, positivity and mass accounting. Zero DF outside the selected initial support is the specified physical distribution, not a post-kick deletion.

The radial phase-space Jacobian and absolute weights are `(2pi)^3*2L*dJr*dL`, with `I=(2E)^(-1/2)`, `Lmax=I-b/I`, `|dJr/dE|=(2E)^(-3/2)`. Independent energy, L, radial-phase and DF/memory-projection refinements are required against the measured mixed response. A known radial echo that survives these checks would validate only this selected cohort's gravitational readout.

Closest mechanism predecessor remains [Chiba et al. (2025), *Galactic echoes*](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16512v2). Their angle/action derivation applies to radial symmetry as well; the potential readout and halo cohort are the tested extension. The 3D gravitational phase-mixing work [Chaturvedi & Luk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14626) supplies broader mathematical context but concerns an external Kepler potential and is not a prediction for this isochrone experiment.

Initial worker allocation remains two CPU-hours total for this branch. Every run has finite endpoints and immutable configuration/source receipts.
