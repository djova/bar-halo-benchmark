# Hidden dynamics: what the early-validation campaign establishes

Final scientific evidence review, 2 October 2026. All scientific trajectories
are closed. This document synthesizes the recorded experiments; it is not
another simulation or an external review. Publication delivery and operational
checks have separate records and do not alter these scientific conclusions.

The campaign's strongest result is a constrained halo-population counterexample.
Two smooth positive distributions can match the initial density, every local
mean velocity and all raw even-total-degree velocity moments, yet absorb
different amounts of angular momentum from the same imposed bar. A new weak-bar
test resolves that difference and supports its expected second-order amplitude
scaling. It does **not** qualify the independently sealed magnitude predictions
to five percent. Collective stability and different live-bar histories remain
unestablished.

This is useful early validation of the hidden-dynamics question. It is not
validation of five galaxy-formation mechanisms. The feedback, echo, collision
and inertia screens narrow their respective hypotheses and expose obstacles
that a larger experiment would have to address.

## The five hypotheses reach different endpoints

| Proposal | Established within the tested model | Stronger claim still missing |
|---|---|---|
| Invisible halo twins | Exact initial moment constraints, positive smooth DFs, and resolved prescribed-bar response differences | Stable, reachable live halos with substantially different naturally evolving bars |
| Selective feedback heating | A controlled cored selectivity example; small resolved cuspy-halo deposits; a qualified warm stellar specific-energy measurement concentrated in the inner guiding cohort | A co-spatial random-motion or stellar-survival bound, or a self-consistent core transformation |
| Halo echoes | A radial signal with timing, scaling and memory-erasure controls; a tiny gravitational readout | A numerically resolved finite spatial echo and measurable stellar structure |
| Collision-law discrimination | Conservative law comparisons and transport-matching controls | A response sign surviving the numerical refinements, let alone a calibrated SIDM discriminator |
| Modified inertia | Solutions of published harmonic equations and distinct noncircular benchmark frequencies at the same circular calibration | General causal evolution, model selection by observations, or a viable replacement for dark matter |

Each row is a different physical model. Passing properties from several rows
cannot be combined into one validated galaxy. None of these new screens has
confronted observed galaxies using a matched measurement and selection pipeline.

## Why the twin result is the most promising lead

The exact construction preserves the reflection-even part of the reference
distribution and cancels its first velocity moments. Higher odd structure
remains free. A bar probes orbital populations through their resonant response,
so initial bulk equality need not imply identical subsequent transfer. See
the [construction and recorded response](research/discovery-20261001/PUBLIC_TWINS_EXACT_SUMMARY.md) and
the [independent proof check](research/discovery-20261001/TWINS_EXACT_MOMENTS_CHALLENGE.md).

For the earlier, stronger prescribed history, the largest tested pair has a
resolved contrast and a point transfer ratio of about 1.45. That ratio has no
qualified confidence interval and falls short of the proposed factor-of-two
ambition. A separate gradually grown history supports another contrast. These
are imposed-field measurements, not examples of galaxies spontaneously growing
different bars.

The subsequent test derives a finite-time second-order forecast from unforced
frequencies, DF derivatives and orbital Fourier coefficients. The forecast was
sealed before evolving the much weaker prescribed bar. An analytic anomaly
calculation repairs a specific pericentre quadrature defect without deleting
radial orbits. The [forecast record](CHECKS_V2.md) retains its
finite-tail and action-grid allowances.

The fixed [Cartesian matrix](TWINS_WEAK.md) evolves eight
independent action libraries with matched phases and two bar amplitudes. All
six transfer differences have positive margins under the declared nominal
family rule. All three paired full-minus-four-half tests support the quadratic
amplitude law. All six magnitude comparisons remain unresolved at five percent:
the action-library uncertainty is much larger than the measured timestep and
phase changes. The p=4 forecast also retains an unqualified tail allowance.
Forecasts lying inside response intervals are not five-percent validations.
No post-outcome sample enlargement or fitted response coefficient is used.

These intervals use nominal Student-t coverage; the refinement allowances are
empirical proxies, not rigorous numerical bounds. An independent saved-data
reader reconstructs their arithmetic but does not repeat orbit integration or
calibrate statistical coverage.

Two further constraints matter for physical interpretation. Short unforced live
screens do not establish collective stability: similar finite-shell moment
flags occur in the same-sample analytic phase controls. Also, exact collisionless
evolution or convex mixing of the exact reference cannot produce these exact
twins because of their quadratic phase-space integral. That excludes one
specific proposed formation route, not every progenitor or approximate twin.

Population-dependent bar response, odd DF freedom and finite-time response
theory have close predecessors. The possible contribution is the combination
of strong explicit constraints, a resolved controlled response and inspectable
forecast limits. Priority still needs specialist criticism; no researchers have
been contacted in this campaign.

## Heating: the population average is part of the physical question

The observable is a specific-energy deposit. It is not a measured change in
stellar random motion. A uniform radial-momentum translation by k adds mean
energy k²/2 to a zero-mean population but leaves its instantaneous radial
velocity variance unchanged. Coherent radial flow, subsequent phase mixing and
local random velocities require separate measurements. Even a qualified energy
cost will not establish a velocity-dispersion or thickness prediction.

The cored positive-gas experiment has a small whole-star/halo-tag specific-energy
ratio, 0.00338. The fixed inner guiding cohort gives 0.08996. This difference
does not require a numerical error: distant stars dilute the whole-population
average. The central stellar hole and the frequency structure also limit the
cored example. See [the cored witness](research/discovery-20261001/FEEDBACK_PUBLIC_SUMMARY.md).

The [centrally populated cusp calculation](research/FEEDBACK_CUSP_FINITE_RESULT.md)
resolves halo-tag heating under two fixed finite gas pulses. The deposits are
only about fifteen and four parts per million of the tag's initial binding
energy; the deepest binding cohort contributes almost none. No core is formed
or demonstrated by such an energy measurement.

The warm stellar calculation supplies a more relevant population but initially
fails an all-state inverse-replay tolerance. A full-support sampling proposal
reduces a rare-path precision problem without changing the physical population.
Selected extended-arithmetic maps identify roundoff sensitivity. The original
all-state extension stops at its cost admission. Both failures remain recorded.

Saved-data analysis finds that the inner guiding cohort contains 2.65% of the
physical stellar mass but contributes about 95.6% and 99.9% of the sampled
heating. These contribution shares preserve numerator–denominator covariance;
they do not by themselves qualify the original failed stellar measurement.

The separately frozen native refinement is now complete. It retains all
initial states, the physical operator and every criterion. Its complete coarse
map exactly matches all seven extended-arithmetic fields and fits the measured
cost admission. Both all-state inverse compositions and all final response,
support, external-work, timestep, action and selected-reference gates pass.
In model units G=M_h=a_h=1, the fine paired specific-energy means at frequencies
ω=5 and 8 are respectively (9.75±0.75)×10⁻⁷ and (1.07±0.13)×10⁻⁷ for the
whole stellar population. For the fixed initial guiding-radius cohort Rc<0.25,
they are (3.51±0.27)×10⁻⁵ and (4.05±0.49)×10⁻⁶ per unit cohort mass. The ±
values are empirical Monte Carlo standard errors, not certified coverage
intervals. These two different denominators are specified parts of the
measurement, not arbitrary renormalizations of samples.

The finishing stage uses approximately 42 complete CPU minutes. It is a
same-sample numerical repair, not an independent physical replication. A saved
reader initially fails exact covariance equality because it regroups a
multiplication and division. Reproducing the original operation order restores
exact equality without changing a tolerance or physical result. Its successful
arithmetic check does not establish general uncertainty coverage.

This qualifies the stated scalar-energy measurement, not a universal no-hide
bound. The stellar guiding cohort and halo binding tag remain different
selections, the field is prescribed and spherical, and no velocity-dispersion
or thickness change is measured. All original failures and cost stops remain
unchanged.
See [the final energy measurement and controls](WARM_COST.md)
and [its exact evidence pointers](WARM_COST.md).

## Memory survives more readily than its visible readout

The [radial echo](research/discovery-20261001/PUBLIC_ECHO_SUMMARY.md) passes independent-formulation, timing,
amplitude and memory-erasure tests. Its force is only about twenty-two parts
per million of the background. An illustrative unamplified stellar readout is
too small to establish visible disk structure. Potential alone is insufficient:
a spherical shell can change an interior potential without exerting an
interior force.

The separate finite angular-sector calculation retains common positive
monopoles in all four shape-sign cases. Its quarter-signed contrast selects the
response odd in both shape perturbations; it is not the original neither/A/B/AB
pulse intervention. This calculation stops despite the finer grid resembling
its leading prediction. Its coarse/fine changes are approximately
29%, 64% and 29% of the respective local scales, failing the declared five-percent
gates. Cross grids identify angular-momentum quadrature as the principal tested
sensitivity. They do not resolve the field. The later timing, memory and
amplitude matrix remains unperformed. See [the retained finite failure](CHECKS_V2.md)
and [its diagnosis](CHECKS_V2.md).

This separates a controlled memory demonstration from the higher-surprise
claim that a three-dimensional halo regenerates visible structure. A larger
experiment would first need a resolved sector and then its collective and
stellar readouts.

## The two shorter screens justify stopping

Transport matching is a useful premise for a collision discriminator, not
evidence that one exists. The cleaner conservative pair matches conditional
drift and the second velocity tensor while differing in a higher moment. Its
initial moving-wave sign does not remain qualified under timestep and cell
refinement. The periodic gas is not a halo or a SIDM model.

The modified-inertia screen solves published harmonic equations. Filters with
the same circular calibration produce substantially different secondary
frequencies. That exposes missing information in circular calibration; neither
numerical residuals nor a noncircular frequency difference establish general
causal dynamics, uniqueness or agreement with observations. Both branches are
terminal; see [their bounded outcomes](research/discovery-20261001/PUBLIC_TERMINAL_BRANCHES.md).

## Advancement and reproducibility

The next scientific campaign should be chosen because it removes one of these
specific ambiguities, not because the current allocation has unused time. For
twins, a new prospective precision design and an equilibrium/stability test
under the actual live force are prerequisites for claims about live bars. For
feedback, a physically appropriate coeval population and self-consistent
energy-to-structure calculation are prerequisites for a core or heating bound.
For echoes, a resolved spatial field precedes amplification and observability.

The [learning record](research/discovery-20261001/LEARNINGS.md), branch protocols, retained failures and
source-pinned results document these decisions. Small public operands support
inspection and arithmetic checks. Access to a saved record is distinct from
complete experimental reproduction; publishing a reader does not make every
private trajectory archive public. Delivery, browser and privacy checks have
their own receipts and do not strengthen the physical result.

The original population-response paper and earlier discovery releases remain
unchanged. This follow-up is an exploratory campaign with bounded results and
explicit non-results. It does not test CDM against SIDM, demonstrate a live
core or bar, validate a replacement gravity theory, or provide an observational
match.
