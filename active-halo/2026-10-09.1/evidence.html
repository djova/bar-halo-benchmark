Matching

What is hidden when the ordinary current is zero

The actual certified halo family has the same density, mean velocity and entire velocity-reversal-even DF as F0. It can still distribute opposite azimuthal currents differently across binding energy and tangential speed. The ordinary current integrates those contributions with equal weight; an energy current or a third velocity moment uses a different weight and exposes their imbalance. These are properties of the existing fixed family, not a new DF or a fit to forced outcomes.

At a point of radius r and polar angle theta, write psi=(1+r²)^(-1/2), R=r sin(theta), e=psi-v²/2 and L²=r²(v_theta²+v_phi²). In units G=M=a=1,

F=C[e^(7/2)(1-Qe²)+R v_phi sum_p c_p h_p],
C=24sqrt(2)/(7pi³), Q=14/33,
h_p=-a_p e^(p-1)+b_p e^(p+1)+L²e^p,
a_p=4p/[(p+5/2)(p+7/2)], b_p=4/(p+1).

The physical mean current is exactly zero power by power. All terms odd under v->-v integrate to zero in every reversal-even statistic. Thus density, pressure, velocity ellipsoid, speed distribution and all other even velocity moments match the reference analytically, while odd higher moments need not. The physical DF stays positive by the unchanged full-support exact primal certificates. The full A, its opposite and the actual separately certified rounded .9A are retained; no moment is used to choose new coefficients.

Direct local integrals

Angular isotropy of the velocity integration measure gives < n_phi² >=1/3, < n_phi² n_t² >=4/15, < n_phi^4 >=1/5 and < n_phi^4 n_t² >=6/35. The full currents are sum_p c_p times these expressions. The reference F0 contributes zero. A single positive power has an energy-current sign change at r²=5/(p-5/2), and a third-moment sign change at r²=4/(p-3/2). Rank one and canonical parity

For a potential input phi, the bare retarded density operator is the density projection of free spherical transport acting on the linear source {F_lambda,phi}. Both free transport and density/Poisson projection are rotation-covariant. The source operator is linear in F. Since L_i h(e,L^2) is an axial rank-one tensor, its three response operators K_i transform as one rank-one tensor; g supplies a scalar operator. Wigner–Eckart selection therefore allows the odd part to connect spatial l only to l-1,l,l+1. This statement applies to arbitrary radial profiles and each retarded time or causal spectral argument, not only diagonal radial matrix elements.

The relevant inversion is the canonical map P:(x,v)->(-x,-v). It leaves e and the axial vector L=x cross v unchanged, so F_lambda is invariant. It preserves the Poisson bracket and the spherical flow; it is not velocity reversal alone. Density projection turns P into ordinary spatial inversion. Consequently the response commutes with spatial parity, and its matrix element between potentials Y_lm and Y_l'm' vanishes when l and l' have opposite parity. Of the rank-one possibilities, l±1 have that forbidden parity. Only l'=l survives. The scalar part already has l'=l by rotational invariance.

The selected Lz direction leaves axial rotations intact, giving m'=m. Within a fixed l the rank-one diagonal element is proportional to m, with an l-specific radial/time operator. Thus every m retains its own operator; zero current does not make their responses isotropic. Negative signed sectors and their causal reflection remain necessary for a real field.

Expand a spatial harmonic along a spherical orbit using its orientation matrix D^l_mk and equatorial factor Y_lk(pi/2,0). Here h_e holds L^2 fixed and the L derivative in actions holds Lz fixed. The nodal term m*h does not disappear because the node frequency is zero.

Unweighted Wigner orthogonality gives delta_ll' for the even and nodal terms. integral_-1^1 |d^l_mk|^2 dmu = 2/(2l+1),

These are the actual affine-DF factors, not an imported isotropic-halo assumption. The k=0/n=0 nodal term stays inside the same l block; it does not invalidate closure or become permission to omit static response.

What closes, and what does not

Poisson self-gravity is diagonal in spatial l,m in this spherical background. Every bare density-response block is already diagonal in l; repeated own-field feedback and dressing cannot open a new l channel. A spatial l=2 source and its self-field therefore form a closed linear field problem, with all five m represented where required. This applies to the forced zero-halo seed and to initial-value forcing whose actual freely projected density has that l. It does not authorize replacing an arbitrary initial DF by such a seed.

A pure spatial-l field is not necessarily a pure simultaneous-rotation phase-space irreducible perturbation. Bracketing L_i h with a spatial-l input can also populate phase-space total-J=l±1 components of the input's parity. Those components have the wrong parity to project onto spatial Y_J and are density-invisible; spherical free transport preserves their J and parity. They must not be silently discarded from an actual DF or canonical energy reconstruction. The precise claim is field closure, not that every induced delta f is a single-total-J phase-space function. For the isotropic F0 energy proof the invariant pure-l phase-space block is a separate, valid stronger restriction.

A higher-rank dependence such as Lz^3, a nonspherical background, an evolved finite-amplitude DF, or the nonlinear bracket {delta f,delta Phi} can permit other spatial l couplings. They are outside this proof. Numerical angular quadrature still has to reproduce the exact contractions; finite-grid cross-block or reality residuals are errors to retain, not identities to force by overwriting fields. Exact l=2 closure neither tests higher-l instabilities nor proves global, nonlinear or continuum stability.
