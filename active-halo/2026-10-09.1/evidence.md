# What is hidden when the ordinary current is zero

The actual certified halo family has the same density, mean velocity and entire
velocity-reversal-even DF as F0. It can still distribute opposite azimuthal
currents differently across binding energy and tangential speed. The ordinary
current integrates those contributions with equal weight; an energy current
or a third velocity moment uses a different weight and exposes their imbalance.
