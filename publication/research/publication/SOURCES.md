# Publication consolidation: source and design review

23 September 2026. This is a focused primary-source check, not a priority survey.

- Elbers, Frenk, Jenkins, Li & Pascoli, *An optimal nonlinear method for simulating relic neutrinos*, MNRAS (2021), DOI10.1093/mnras/stab2260; inspected preprint2010.07321, sections2–2.3. The delta-f decomposition and weighted particles estimate a perturbation relative to a reference DF; the authors explicitly derive a control-variate interpretation. This is relevant to estimator positioning. Our cumulative impulse identity is a different construction with more restrictive external-map assumptions; equivalence and priority remain open.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.07321
- W3C WAI, Complex Images: short identification plus a readable account of essential values, relationships and trends. The article provides complete tables and captions; controls enhance them. Automated/mobile checks alone do not establish full accessibility conformance.
  https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/

  https://www.gofair.foundation/fair-principles
