What matters scientifically?

The organizing question is how a physical model earns explanatory power. A convincing image is a starting point. We need mechanisms, controlled contrasts, reliable calculations and a defensible connection to observed data.

Distinguish causes

Vary numerical settings separately from physical assumptions. A stable aggregate or a conserved quantity can conceal inaccurate individual predictions.

van den Bosch & Ogiya: numerical disruption · Chiang et al.: extended convergence tests

Look beneath the density

Orbital populations and their distribution in phase space can change the dynamics even when the density profiles look alike.

Chiang et al.: anisotropic subhaloes · Dattathri et al.: core dynamics

Model the measurement

Observed light and motion constrain hidden matter through assumptions, selection and estimators. Test that chain against known inputs.

van den Bosch et al.: Basilisk I · Mitra & van den Bosch: model flexibility

Connect reservoirs and timescales

Galaxies exchange mass, energy and angular momentum with their surroundings. Cooling, mixing, accretion and assembly can preserve a history that one final image cannot identify.

Mandelker et al.: cooling streams · Aung et al.: entrainment and galaxy fueling

The browser design is our adaptation, not a style prescribed by the papers. The goal is to let readers change a meaningful assumption or reference frame and see what that changes in the inference. Decorative motion and invented simulation frames would work against that goal.

The practical writing pattern is: physical question → competing explanations → operational definition → controlled contrast → measured result → bounded implication. The site puts the key result first and defers derivations, complete parameter grids and troubleshooting to linked evidence pages.

Earlier interactive-design review · 21 September 2026

On 21 September 2026, the author-disambiguated search identified 77 arXiv papers submitted from September 2016 through the review date. All 77 PDFs were retrieved (1,557 pages). Broad screening examined abstract excerpts and selected full-text passages; 14 sources received focused passage/caption review, and seven original figure pages were inspected visually.

This is not a claim that all 1,557 pages were read line by line, or that every journal publication was discovered. The search may miss unindexed items. The corpus includes white papers and one general physics-benchmark paper. Primary PDFs were used for the actual conceptual review; third-party author metrics were not treated as evidence about scientific quality.
