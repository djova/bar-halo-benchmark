# Focused scholarly positioning, 23 September 2026

This is a targeted primary-source check, not an exhaustive novelty or priority
search. It supplements the prior population-response literature record.

- [Hamilton et al., Galactic bar resonances with diffusion](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.03855v2),
  sectionsIII.2 andIV: stationary resonant diffusion and a local linear DF are
  already part of the literature. Our population counterexample is not the
  discovery that a local approximation has limitations.
- [Chiba, Dynamical friction and feedback on galactic bars in the general
  fast-slow regime](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.00022v2), abstract and discussion:
  the collisionless averaged dynamics is compared with3Dcalculations. The
  discussion explicitly anticipates diffusion restoring friction-producing
  gradients while eroding the trapped/untrapped density contrast. Opposing
  diffusion effects are therefore not our novelty claim.
- [Ogilvie & Lubow, The effect of planetary migration on the corotation
  resonance](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0605138),2006; [journal version](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10506.x):
  a close migration/diffusion analogue, including a trapped-region anomaly and
  its viscous limitation, in a gaseous-disc problem with different physics.
- [Dellaportas & Kontoyiannis, Control variates for estimation based on
  reversible Markov chain Monte Carlo samplers](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article-abstract/74/1/133/7074993),2012:
  explicit control-variate construction, asymptotic variance and optimal
  combinations in reversible MCMC. This is context for an established numerical
  principle, not an assertion of equivalence to our finite-time canonical map.
- [Alexopoulos, Dellaportas & Titsias, Variance reduction for Metropolis–Hastings
  samplers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02268): another primary example of useful
  variance reduction through approximate Poisson-equation controls. It does not
  establish priority for our cumulative construction.

Full mathematical equivalence of the cumulative identity to prior δf or other
control-variate constructions remains a concrete external-review question.
Search results and a few inspected primary sources cannot prove originality.
No external scientist has been contacted as part of this campaign.
